# CSU brand marks

The CSU web guidelines (brand.colostate.edu/web-guidelines) require the university signature in
the green header and the wordmark in the green footer. Put the official reversed (all white)
files from https://brand.colostate.edu/signature/ here, with these names:

- `csu-signature-reversed.svg`  (header, links to colostate.edu)
- `csu-wordmark-reversed.svg`   (footer, links to colostate.edu)

PNG works too: change the two names at the top of `js/site.js`. Do not edit, recolour or
shrink the artwork (the brand pages forbid it). Until the files are here, the pages show the
words "Colorado State University" in the same place.
